Burger Steak

Description

Ingredients

  • 1/2 kilo ground pork
  • 1/2 kilo ground beef
  • 3/4 cup bread crumbs
  • 2 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 piece white onion minced
  • 5 cloves garlic minced
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 2 tsp. salt
  • 1 piece raw egg
  • 1 tsp. sugar
For Mushroom Gravy:
  • 2 Tbsp. oil from fried patties
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup sliced button mushroom
  • 2 cups beef broth 1 beef cube dissolved in 2 cups water
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine ground pork, ground beef, bread crumbs, milk, Worcestershire sauce, onion, garlic, black pepper, salt, egg and sugar.
  2. Mix thoroughly and form into patties on a wax paper. Freeze for at least 30 minutes or more.
  3. In a non-stick pan or stove top grill, cook the patties for at least 3 minutes on each sides or until golden brown. Transfer to a serving dish and set aside to assemble late with the mushroom sauce.
  4. To make the mushroom sauce, saute the mushroom in a pan with a little oil until all sides are seared. Set aside.
  5. Heat a pan and put the oil and butter. Melt the butter until it is hot enough and test by sprinkling a little flour and when it bubbles, it is ready. Then put in the flour. Stir until the mixture turns light brown and turns into a roux or paste.
  6. Pour in the beef broth, salt and pepper and bring to a boil. Stir until it turns into a thick gravy. Put in the mushrooms and stir for a minute
  7. Pour over the burger patties and serve with steamed rice. Enjoy!
